How to use these formulas
1. One-time auth: Paste =IMPORTRANGE("MASTER_URL","Transactions!A1") into any cell in a new subsidiary sheet and click "Allow access" when prompted. Do this once per sheet.
2. B3 = Month cell: All current-month formulas use B3 as the month filter (e.g. "Mar 2026"). Put your month label in B3.
3. Paste as formula: Click the green formula to select it, then copy and paste into Google Sheets. It becomes live when pasted.
4. Dedicated N/P pairs: 10X Roofing, HRE, and CardoMax each have two pairs — a Due To/From pair (25001/15152) and a Promissory Note pair.
