How to pull these reports from Shopify ▸
1 — Orders Export
  1. In Shopify Admin, open Orders in the left sidebar.
  2. Filter by date: click the date filter and choose a custom range (e.g. Jul 16–31, 2026). Apply.
  3. Click the Export button (top-right corner).
  4. Select Orders matching filters and format CSV for Excel, then click Export orders.
  5. Shopify emails you the file, or it downloads automatically. Rename if needed — any .csv works.
2 — Payment Transactions Export
  1. In Shopify Admin, open Finances in the left sidebar, then click Payments.
  2. Click Export (top-right corner).
  3. Set the date range to match your orders period.
  4. Click Export transactions. The file downloads as payment_transactions_export.csv.
  5. This tool uses only charge-type rows from this file to map Payout IDs — no other rows are imported.
